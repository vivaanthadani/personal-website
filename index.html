About

Hands-on, analytical, and field-tested.

I'm a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University with a 3.83 cGPA. I like problems that start open-ended and end with a physical part that works: something I've modelled, analysed, built, broken, and improved.

This past summer at Toronto Water, I designed and deployed hardware for live sewer and stormwater infrastructure. That included a solar-powered, Arduino-driven camera lens wiper that saves about $6,000 a year per site, custom sensor caps, and TPU crawler wheels. I also flew a Flyability Elios 3 LiDAR drone to map confined spaces and wrote the report that set the team's LiDAR target standard.

On TMU Hyperloop I'm the Structures Project Lead. I run static structural FEA in ANSYS on LIM mounts and aluminium brackets, I designed a NACA duct that we validated in the wind tunnel, and I lead R&D on a PCM-assisted water–glycol cooling system for the linear induction motors.

I'm looking for a 2027 co-op term in mechanical design, product development, R&D, manufacturing, or energy and infrastructure, where I can contribute from the first week and keep learning fast.
