Background

About Me & Skills

My journey in mechanical engineering stems from a fundamental curiosity about how systems function, collaborate, and persist under stress. By investigating thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, material science, and kinematics, I aim to design components that are structurally optimized and practical to manufacture.

I love bridging the gap between digital theory and physical product creation. I frequently translate my ideas into detailed SolidWorks CAD assemblies, perform structural optimization using FEA tools, and prototype systems with Arduinos, 3D printers, and custom-milled boards.

"I am constantly looking for the next problem to dissect and solve, whether that means calculating static load lines, designing an IoT device enclosure, or refining complex assemblies."
